6. Parking Fee Settlement and Credit Buffer 
6.1. All incurred parking fees and charges resulting from recorded parking sessions 
must be paid manually by you within the Metre App using Faster Payment System (FPS) 
or other manual payment methods approved by us from time to time. 
6.2. Seamless Exit Buffer: To enable seamless barrier clearance, we grant you an 
accumulated unpaid parking fee buffer limit of up to HK$100 (or such other limit as we 
may announce from time to time). 
6.3. As long as your total outstanding, unpaid parking fees do not exceed the HK$100 
buffer limit, you are permitted to exit participating parking facilities immediately upon 
session completion without paying on the spot at the exit barrier. 
6.4. You are required to settle all outstanding parking fees via FPS in the Metre App in a 
timely manner. If a new or ongoing parking session causes your total accumulated 
unpaid balance to equal or exceed HK$100, you will not be eligible for seamless exit, 
and your MetreTag access may be temporarily restricted until all pending balances are 
fully settled via FPS in the Metre App. 
6.5. We reserve the right to adjust, suspend, or revoke your buffer limit, or apply 
reasonable administrative late payment charges as published in our Schedule of Fees 
and Guidelines. Save where such adjustment, suspension, or revocation is triggered by 
your non-payment, default, or breach of these Conditions of Issue, we will provide you 
with at least 7 days' prior notice via the Metre App before any reduction or revocation of 
your buffer limit takes effect.
