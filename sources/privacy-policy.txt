SUMMARY OF THIS PRIVACY POLICY STATEMENT 
This summary highlights the key points of our privacy practices for your convenience. It is not a 
substitute for the full Privacy Policy Statement below, which you should read in full and which 
prevails over this summary in the event of any inconsistency. 
• What we collect: your name and contact details, payment/settlement identifiers, MetreTag and 
device identifiers, and MetreTag usage/parking session data. We do not collect credit or debit card 
numbers, and we do not use cookies, tracking technologies, or SDKs to collect data (paragraphs 4–
5). 
• Why we use it: to issue and manage your MetreTag, operate our parking access and payment 
systems, provide customer support, and meet our legal and compliance obligations (paragraph 6). 
• Marketing: we only send marketing communications with your explicit consent, and you can opt 
out at any time, free of charge (paragraphs 7–8). 
• Who we share it with: infrastructure and technical service providers, participating parking 
operators, operational contractors, our affiliates, and regulators or law enforcement where legally 
required — all within Hong Kong (paragraph 9). 
• How long we keep it: only for as long as necessary, after which it is erased or anonymized 
(paragraphs 11–13). 
• Security: we apply technical, organizational, and managerial safeguards to protect your data 
(paragraphs 14–16). 
• Children: our services are not intended for, and we do not knowingly collect data from, persons 
under 18 (paragraph 17). 
• Data breaches: we are committed to notifying affected users and the Privacy Commissioner for 
Personal Data of a significant data breach (paragraph 18). 
• Automated decisions: certain fraud-prevention decisions may be automated; you may request 
human review (paragraph 19). 
• Your rights: access, correction, opt-out of marketing, and complaint to the Privacy Commissioner 
for Personal Data (see the summary table and paragraphs 20–25).

GENERAL 
1. Metre Hong Kong Limited (the "Company", "we", "our" or "us"), the issuer of the MetreTag 
and operator of the Metre App and associated platforms, respects the privacy of its users and 
recognizes the importance of protecting personal data. We collect and process personal data 
to efficiently provide our vehicle access, parking session tracking, and transaction settlement 
services to our customers. This Privacy Policy Statement is designed to help you understand 
what information the Company collects, how we use and safeguard that information, and your 
rights regarding your personal data. 
DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION 
2. In this Privacy Policy Statement, unless the context otherwise requires: 
"Ordinance" means the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of the Laws of the 
Hong Kong Special Administrative Region; 
"Personal Data" means any personal data held by the Company that falls within the 
following categories, in each case to the extent that it constitutes “personal data” under 
section 2(1) of the Ordinance: 
(a) Identity and Registration Data — information provided by an applicant, account 
holder, or registered user of the Metre App or MetreTag, and information relating to 
persons linked to a Service Provider or commercial partner applicant (including 
beneficial owners, directors, and officers of corporate entities), whether contained in 
registration forms or submitted via the Metre App; 
(b) Session Data — data and records collected during your use of the MetreTag, 
including parking session entry and exit timestamps, physical locations, and session 
duration data captured by our Metre readers or integrated hardware systems; 
(c) Account and Payment Data — data and records collected during the operation and 
use of the Metre App and payment processing services, including linked payment 
details, account transaction logs, and device identifiers; and 
(d) Device Data — information relating to the mobile device(s) used to access the Metre 
App, as further described in paragraph 4(f) below; 
"Service Provider" means any car park operator, property manager, access control operator, 
or mobility service provider that utilizes the Metre system to record parking sessions, grant 
vehicle access, or manage parking premises, and has been approved by us. Service Providers 
will clearly display the Metre acceptance logo at their physical locations, access barriers, or 
within online channels. 
OUR COMPANY POLICY 
3. We pledge to comply fully with, and where reasonably practicable exceed, the requirements 
of the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of Hong Kong. In fulfilling this pledge, 
we commit to: 
(i)  Collect adequate, but not excessive, Personal Data by lawful and fair means solely 
for lawful purposes directly related to our vehicle access, parking session tracking, 
payment settlement, and account administration operations; 
(ii)  Take all reasonably practicable steps to ensure that all Personal Data collected or 
retained by us is accurate and kept up to date, having regard to the purposes for 
which it is used;

that the data is inaccurate or outdated, having regard to the specified operational

TYPES OF PERSONAL DATA COLLECTED AND HELD 
4. For the purpose of conducting the Company's business (including operating the Metre App, 
processing vehicle access, and settling parking transactions), we may collect from you and 
hold some or all of the following Personal Data (depending on the specific products, features, 
or services you select) to enable us to issue your MetreTag and provide our services to you: 
a. Your name; 
b. Contact details, including email address, and delivery or mailing address (for 
MetreTag dispatch and official correspondence); 
c. Payment and settlement information, including designated FPS (Faster Payment 
System) identifiers, proxy IDs, or bank account details for processing deposit refunds 
or transaction settlements. We do not collect or store credit or debit card numbers; 
d. Your MetreTag identifier, hardware serial number, and cryptographic binding keys 
associated with your account; 
e. MetreTag usage and session data, including parking entry/exit timestamps, session 
duration, physical car park locations, and barrier interactions1; 
f. Mobile device information, including unique device identifiers (e.g., IDFV/UUID), 
operating system version, and device parameters where the Metre App is installed; 
g. Customer support records, including app activity logs, feedback submissions, and 
communication history between you and our support channels.

PURPOSES OF KEEPING PERSONAL DATA 
6. Personal Data held by us may be used for any of the following purposes (as applicable): 
a. Processing your account registration and orders for On-Loan MetreTags; 
b. Managing, operating, and maintaining the Metre parking access system, Metre App, 
and hardware infrastructure, including processing entry/exit barrier clearance and 
calculating session fees; 
c. Verifying your MetreTag serials, payment credentials, and app activity records; 
d. Processing payment settlements, collecting outstanding parking fees, and managing 
FPS Deposit transfers or refunds; 
e. Sending you operational communications, including real-time parking session 
notifications, fee statements, system alerts, and customer support messages; 
f. Providing you with direct marketing materials and promotions (subject to your 
explicit consent as set out in paragraph 8); 
g. Enforcing our legal rights under the Conditions of Issue, including investigating 
improper use, non-payment, or unreturned On-Loan MetreTags; 
h. Conducting customer due diligence, risk assessments, and compliance checks as 
required by applicable laws, rules, or guidelines in Hong Kong; 
i. Designing new products or improving existing hardware, firmware, mobile 
applications, and parking ecosystem services provided by us, our subsidiaries, and 
our affiliates, based on the analysis of anonymized and/or aggregated data that does 
not identify you; 
j. Investigating customer complaints, technical glitches, hardware malfunctions, or 
suspicious transaction activities; 
k. Preventing, detecting, or investigating fraud, unauthorized device modification, or 
criminal activities; and 
l. Disclosing information as required by any applicable laws, court orders, regulatory 
authorities, or law enforcement requests within the Hong Kong Special 
Administrative Region.

our products, services, special promotions, and offers relating to parking and mobility 
services: 
a. Data Used: Your name, email address, and mobile device identifiers (for in-app push 
notifications). We do not collect or use telephone numbers for direct marketing 
purposes; 
b. Delivery Channels: Email and push notifications delivered through the Metre App; 
c. Consent Required: We will not use your Personal Data for direct marketing 
purposes unless we have received your explicit prior consent. You may opt out or

DISCLOSURE OF PERSONAL DATA 
9. All Personal Data held by us will be kept confidential. However, for the purposes set out in 
paragraphs 6 and 7 above, we may transfer or disclose such Personal Data to the following 
parties within Hong Kong: 
a. Infrastructure & Technical Service Providers: Third-party cloud service providers, 
IT system hosts, software developers, and database management contractors bound 
by a duty of confidentiality to us; 
b. Parking Operators: Participating car park owners, operators, and property 
management companies bound by a duty of confidentiality to us, solely to the extent 
necessary to verify vehicle entry/exit, clear access barriers, and resolve session 
discrepancies; 
c. Operational Contractors: Our agents, contractors, and service providers who owe a 
duty of confidentiality to us and provide administrative, logistics/courier (for 
MetreTag delivery), payment execution (such as FPS gateway providers), debt 
collection (in the event of overdue parking fees), legal, auditing, or customer support 
services in connection with our business operations; 
d. Affiliates: Our subsidiaries and direct holding company which owe a duty of 
confidentiality to us for internal management and service execution; and 
e. Regulatory & Legal Authorities: Any law enforcement agencies, government 
bodies, regulatory authorities, or courts of law in Hong Kong to whom we, our 
subsidiaries, or our affiliates are under a legally binding obligation or court order to 
satisfy a disclosure demand under applicable laws and regulations. 
10. For the avoidance of doubt, we will not transfer or disclose your Personal Data to any third 
party (including our subsidiaries or affiliates) for use in direct marketing by that third party 
unless we have received your explicit prior written or in-app consent.

11. Personal Data collected and maintained by the Company shall be as accurate, complete, and 
up-to-date as is necessary for the performance of our parking access and transaction 
settlement services. 
12. Personal Data will not be retained longer than necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it 
was collected. Data that is no longer required will be erased, unless retention is required under

13. We destroy records containing Personal Data in accordance with our internal security

SECURITY OF PERSONAL DATA 
14. We prioritize data security and implement appropriate technical, organizational, and 
managerial safeguards to protect your Personal Data against unauthorized or accidental 
access, processing, loss, or erasure. 
15. Our cloud infrastructure, databases, and APIs are protected by industry-standard firewalls, 
encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS), and hardware-level cryptographic controls. As standard 
email transmissions are not fully secure, sensitive personal credentials or verification 
documents should only be submitted through secure upload mechanisms within the Metre 
App or designated support channels. 
16. Unnecessary or obsolete account data will be purged from our active systems in accordance 
with our internal retention schedules. 
CHILDREN'S PERSONAL DATA 
17. The Metre App and our services are not intended for, or directed at, persons under the age of 
18, and we do not knowingly collect Personal Data from persons under the age of 18. If we 
become aware that we have inadvertently collected Personal Data from a person under the age 
of 18 without the necessary consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete that data as soon 
as practicable. If you believe that a person under the age of 18 has provided us with Personal 
Data, please contact our Data Protection Officer using the contact details set out in paragraph 
25 below. 
DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION 
18. While the Ordinance does not currently impose a mandatory requirement on us to notify 
affected individuals or the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (“PCPD”) of a data 
breach, we are committed to good practice in this area, having regard to the PCPD's Guidance 
on Data Breach Handling and the Giving of Breach Notifications. In the event of a data 
breach involving your Personal Data that poses a real risk of harm to you, we will, as soon as 
reasonably practicable: (a) take immediate steps to contain the breach and mitigate any 
resulting harm; (b) assess the cause, extent, and impact of the breach; and (c) notify affected 
users, and, where appropriate, the PCPD, of the breach and of the steps we have taken or 
intend to take in response, in line with applicable best practice. 
AUTOMATED DECISION-MAKING 
19. In limited circumstances, we may use automated tools or systems to support decisions that 
affect you — for example, algorithmic checks used to detect suspected fraud or unauthorized 
use of a MetreTag or account (see paragraph 6(k) above), which may result in a temporary 
suspension of your account or MetreTag pending further review. Where an automated 
decision has a legal or otherwise significant effect on you, you may request that a member of 
our staff review that decision by contacting our Data Protection Officer using the contact 
details set out in paragraph 25 below, and we will give you the opportunity to present your 
views before a final decision is made.

20. You have the right to request access to any Personal Data we hold about you. To submit a 
Data Access Request, please complete the prescribed form issued by the Privacy 
Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD) and send it, along with proof of identity, to our Data 
Protection Officer at the address set out in paragraph 25 below or via dpo@metre.hk. We may 
charge a reasonable fee permitted under the Ordinance to cover administrative costs for 
processing your request. 
21. You have the right to object to or opt out of our use of your Personal Data for direct 
marketing purposes at any time, free of charge. You can manage your preferences directly in 
the Metre App settings or by notifying us via our official support channels. 
22. You have the right to request the correction of any inaccurate Personal Data held by us by 
contacting our Data Protection Officer. 
23. We will comply with your data access or correction request unless we are required or 
permitted to refuse the request under the Ordinance or other applicable legal grounds. 
24. If you believe that we have handled your Personal Data in a manner inconsistent with this 
Privacy Policy Statement or the Ordinance, we encourage you to contact our Data Protection 
Officer in the first instance (see paragraph 25 below) so that we may investigate and address 
your concerns. You also have the right to lodge a complaint directly with the Office of the 
Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data, Hong Kong (Hotline: 2827 2827; Website: 
www.pcpd.org.hk), which independently oversees compliance with the Ordinance.

AMENDMENTS TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY STATEMENT 
27. We keep this Privacy Policy Statement under regular review. Where we make any material 
change to this Privacy Policy Statement, we will give you reasonable advance notice of not 
less than 30 days before the change takes effect, and will notify you by email and/or in-app 
notification via the Metre App, in addition to posting the updated Privacy Policy Statement on 
our official website (www.metre.hk). Minor or non-material changes (for example, 
corrections of typographical errors) may be made without prior notice. 
INTERNAL COMPLIANCE PRACTICES 
28. Our Data Protection Officer oversees compliance with the Ordinance within the Company. 
We maintain the following internal measures: 
a. Maintenance of a formal log book recording any refused data access or correction 
requests and the specific legal grounds for refusal; 
b. Inclusion of a clear Personal Information Collection Statement (PICS) in our app 
onboarding and account registration workflows; and 
c. Implementation of internal privacy guidelines, data security protocols, and staff 
training to ensure alignment with up-to-date data protection standards.
